GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What should a business video cost?

Costs vary with the concept, crew, locations, shoot days, animation, and edit requirements. Ask for a scoped estimate that names deliverables and revision rounds.

How long does production take?

A straightforward project may take a few weeks from briefing to delivery. Scheduling, stakeholder approvals, locations, and complex edits can extend the timeline.

Which video should a business make first?

Choose the video that answers a real customer question or supports a near-term business goal. A concise service explainer or customer story is often a practical starting point.
